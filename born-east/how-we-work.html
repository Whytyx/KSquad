How We Work

Support That Suits Your Business.

Every business is different. Some need guidance on one issue, others have a project to complete, and some need assistance throughout the year. We offer three ways to work together.

01

Advice & Consultation

For when you need to talk through a particular issue, understand your options and decide what to do next. For example, a restaurant owner seeking guidance on a staff performance concern before deciding how to proceed.

02

Project Support

For when you need a defined piece of work completed. This could mean supporting an employee relations case, preparing HR documents, organising food safety records or developing a business action plan.

03

Ongoing Support

For businesses that need regular assistance with agreed activities, such as external HR advisory support or ongoing documentation and customer communication work.

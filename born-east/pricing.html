How these prices work

  • We work Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, excluding bank holidays.
  • For Remote HR Advisor, we first reply within 2 working days. For other retainers, we first reply within 1 working day. A first reply is not the same as resolving the matter. We do not provide emergency cover.
  • Up to 2 nominated management contacts can reach us. Employee access for Outsourced HR is agreed separately.
  • For Remote HR Advisor, routine queries and brief follow-ups are included under fair use. Substantial follow-up or detailed casework is quoted in advance. For other retainers, unused allowance does not roll over.
  • Remote HR Advisor covers phone and email guidance. Drafting letters, document reviews and detailed casework are scoped separately, or included in Practical HR Support.
  • Half-day support includes preparation, review and reporting. Extra work or travel is agreed first. Formal investigations, hearing delivery and existing cases are scoped separately. You approve the decisions for your business.
  • These are not included unless we agree them separately: payroll processing, recruitment campaigns, specialist legal or immigration advice, and tribunal representation.

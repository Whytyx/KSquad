    Monthly plans roll month to month. Cancel with 30 days’ notice.

  • 02What if I need something not listed?

    Tell us on a free call and we’ll quote it.

  • 03Do you give legal advice?

    We are not solicitors. For complex legal matters we’ll tell you when to get legal advice.

    Our food safety service is admin only: setting up records and checking they are complete, about 1–1.5 hours a month. HR support covers up to 2–4 hours a month of advice, letters and record-keeping with more responsibility attached, so it costs more.

  • 05What happens if I need more hours in a month?

    We tell you before going over. Extra time is £60 an hour, only if you agree first.

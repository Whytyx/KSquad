Food Safety Support

Less Paperwork. More Time for Your Business.

Food safety records are part of running a food business, but keeping them complete takes time. We handle the admin side for restaurants, cafés, pubs and takeaways: setting up your records, keeping them complete and ready to show, and following up with your team.

Sound familiar?

  • “An EHO inspection is coming up and I’m not sure what paperwork I should have ready.”

  • “By Friday, half the fridge temperature checks are still blank.”

  1. 01

    Records are on loose sheets, or spread across notebooks and phones.

    What we do

    We set up one organised system for your temperature logs, cleaning schedules, delivery checks and allergen information, based on the food safety procedures you already have.

  2. 02

    Checks get missed because nobody is sure whose job they are.

    What we do

    We write simple checklists and step-by-step procedures (SOPs) that show who does each check, when, and where it is recorded.

  3. 03

    You’re not sure your records are complete if an officer asks to see them.

    What we do

    We check that records are filled in, dated and signed, flag any gaps to you, and keep the file in order so you can find what you need.

    Staff forget to fill in records, and the allergen matrix falls behind menu changes.

    What we do

    With monthly support, we follow up with your team on missed records, update the allergen matrix from the ingredient information you send us, and keep the training tracker current.

The legal side

Food Safety Is a Legal Requirement

In the UK, food safety and hygiene are legal requirements for every food business, from restaurants and cafés to pubs and takeaways.

  • Food business registration

    Food businesses need to register with their local authority before they start trading.

  • HACCP-based food safety management

    You need food safety procedures based on HACCP principles, records that show they are being followed, and staff who are trained or supervised in food hygiene.

  • Food hygiene ratings

    Local authority food safety officers inspect food businesses. In England, Wales and Northern Ireland the result is a food hygiene rating that customers can see; Scotland has its own Food Hygiene Information Scheme.

Where we help: we handle the admin side: record-keeping, checklists and follow-up, built around the food safety procedures you already have, such as your Safer Food, Better Business pack or HACCP plan. Those procedures need to come from you or a qualified food safety professional. We are not inspectors or an official body, and we can’t promise any inspection result. Requirements can vary, so check the details for your business with your local authority or the Food Standards Agency (Food Standards Scotland in Scotland).

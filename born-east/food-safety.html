The legal side

Food Safety Is a Legal Requirement

In the UK, food safety and hygiene are legal requirements for every food business, from restaurants and cafés to pubs and takeaways.

  • Food business registration

    Food businesses need to register with their local authority before they start trading.

  • HACCP-based food safety management

    You need food safety procedures based on HACCP principles, records that show they are being followed, and staff who are trained or supervised in food hygiene.

  • Food hygiene ratings

    Local authority food safety officers inspect food businesses. In England, Wales and Northern Ireland the result is a food hygiene rating that customers can see; Scotland has its own Food Hygiene Information Scheme.

Where we help: we help you get organised and stay on top of the day-to-day paperwork, with clear records based on your procedures, coordinated staff training and regular follow-up, so you’re better prepared when an inspection comes. We are not inspectors or an official body. Requirements can vary, so check the details for your business with your local authority or the Food Standards Agency (Food Standards Scotland in Scotland).

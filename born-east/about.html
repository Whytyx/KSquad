A Little About Born East Solutions.

Born East Solutions was created to offer independent business owners a straightforward way to access support with the responsibilities that come with running a business.

With a background in HR and business management, we focus on understanding each client’s situation, agreeing what we can do and working with them from there.

We’re particularly interested in independent hospitality businesses, where people matters, daily operations and customer expectations often compete for attention. We also work with other small businesses when our services are relevant.

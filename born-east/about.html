About

Experienced HR Leadership. Part of Your Team.

understanding first, then working together

Born East Solutions was founded to help independent businesses manage the people, paperwork and everyday operational challenges that can take valuable time away from running their business.

With over 15 years of experience in hospitality, including senior HR leadership within a multi-site restaurant business, our founder brings first-hand experience of employee relations, HR compliance, people operations and business support.

We understand the pressures of managing teams, maintaining standards and dealing with unexpected issues, because we’ve experienced them firsthand.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the problem, agree what needs to be done, then take care of it for you so you can get on with running your business.

A note from our founder

Gayle

Founder & Business Consultant

That is why I founded Born East Solutions.

  1. Why it started

    Running a business means wearing many hats. You manage people, handle day-to-day challenges, keep up with regulations and make sure everything runs smoothly. There is always something that needs attention, but not every business has the resources, or the need, to employ someone for every area of support.

  2. What I wanted

    I wanted to give business owners access to experienced support without the cost and commitment of hiring more staff or building an in-house team. Someone to turn to when you need advice, an extra pair of hands to get things done, or ongoing support as your business grows.

  3. My background

    My background is in people management, employee relations and business support. Over my career I have managed workplace challenges, supported teams, improved processes and helped businesses work through both operational and people matters.

  4. What Born East does

    Through Born East, I bring that experience together to support businesses with people management, employment compliance, food safety, day-to-day operations and customer experience, with a particular focus on hospitality.

  5. How I work

    I believe the best support starts with understanding how your business actually works. Every business has its own priorities, challenges and ways of doing things, so I listen first, understand what you need, and find solutions that fit the way you work.

Whether you are facing a challenge, dealing with something you have been putting off, or planning your next venture, let’s talk. Tell me what you are working through or hoping to achieve, and let’s see how I can take some of the pressure off, so you can focus on what you do best: running and growing your business.

Gayle

About

Experienced HR Leadership. Practical Business Support.

understanding first, then working together

Born East Solutions was founded to help independent businesses manage the people, paperwork and everyday operational challenges that can take valuable time away from running their business.

With over 13 years of experience in hospitality, including senior HR leadership within a multi-site restaurant business, our founder brings practical experience in employee relations, HR compliance, people operations and business support.

We understand the pressures of managing teams, maintaining standards and dealing with unexpected issues — because we’ve experienced them firsthand.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the problem, agree what needs to be done and provide practical support to move things forward.

Where this comes from

Over 13 years in a growing hospitality business — starting in operations, moving into a combined HR and operations role, and helping lead the people team across more than 40 sites alongside the People Director.

That route matters more than the job titles. I’ve sat on both sides of the table: the side that needs the shift covered tonight, and the side that needs it done properly.

How I work

I don’t think every business needs complicated systems, lengthy policies or expensive consultancy packages. You need the right level of HR for the business you actually have.

So the approach is simple: understand your business, find the problem or the risk, explain your options in plain English, and help you put something practical in place. No jargon, no hedging, and nothing you’ll never read.

What I’m aiming for

To make employing people easier, safer and less stressful — so you can get back to running your business.
